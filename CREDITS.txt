TARDIS exterior (models/tardis/default.glb)
  Original: "TARDIS.fbx" from the mod author's own collection (a closed box of four identical sides).
  Author and licence are NOT recorded in the file: fill them in before publishing.
  Changes: scaled to 2.3 m, origin on the floor in the middle of the base, the right leaf of one side
           split off as an openable door (origin on its hinge), lettering decimated (31 000 to 12 000
           triangles), parts of one material joined.

10th Doctor's console room (models/interior/10.glb, 10.json)
  Original: "TARDISY V2 Beta1" Blender file, by Krauterz (the interior; its exterior scene is by
  TARDISMAN and is not used). Free to use "for any of your projects, commercial or otherwise", on
  condition that Krauterz is credited for the interior in the credits of any game that uses it: this
  file is that credit. (The author's guide also thanks PulpDood for the original concept.)
  Changes: made for walking in on a Quest 3 instead of for rendering: 4.1 million triangles reduced to
  about 230 000 (decimated; the pillars from the shape under their geometry nodes), 116 node-built
  materials replaced by 62 plain ones (base-colour textures kept at 512 px, the rest colours), the
  lights, the cameras and the render shell dropped, small parts and the roundels' detail lost, the whole
  moved so the floor is at 0. It is a coarse version of a detailed model.

1st Doctor's console room (models/interior/1.glb, 1.json)
  Original: "type 30 tardis animated.fbx" (src.local/interior/1/type-30-tardis-animated.zip, from the mod
  author's own collection; a downloaded model of the 1963 set). Its author and licence are NOT recorded in
  the file or the archive (no readme came with it): fill them in before publishing.
  Changes: the units brought to metres (the FBX is 1/55 of a room), 2 290 empty transforms dropped, the
  console decimated (158 000 to 28 000 triangles), 24 flat lambert materials replaced by 15 plain ones
  (the console tinted pale green, the roundels white, the rotor's tube made clear, the door given a colour
  of its own), the origin put on the floor under the console. The model has no textures.
